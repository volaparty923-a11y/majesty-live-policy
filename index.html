Majesty Live Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Majesty Live is a social voice-chat application. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, shared, and protected when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may process account details such as your user ID, username, and profile photo; voice-room activity and content you submit; room participation, gifts, virtual diamond balances, and transactions; technical and diagnostic information; and information you provide when contacting support.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate accounts and voice rooms, provide app features, maintain virtual balances and transactions, prevent fraud and abuse, respond to support requests, improve reliability, and meet legal obligations.

3. Microphone Access

The app may request microphone access so you can speak in voice rooms. Audio handling, recording, and retention depend on the app's actual implementation. Please ensure this description accurately reflects how the app works.

4. Service Providers and Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers used by the app, such as authentication, database, voice communication, hosting, analytics, or payment providers, where integrated. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect users and the service.

5. Data Retention and Security

Information should be retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to legal and security requirements. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Majesty Live is not intended for children under 13, or any higher minimum age required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can investigate.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information. Contact us to make a request.

8. Third-Party Services

Third-party services integrated into the app may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact Us
