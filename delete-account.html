Majesty Live Account Deletion

Users who have created a Majesty Live account can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to volaparty923@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject "Majesty Live Account Deletion".
  3. Include your Majesty Live user ID and the email address associated with your account, if applicable.

We will verify the request and explain any additional steps needed to confirm your identity.

Data deletion

After verifying a valid request, we will delete the account and associated personal data that we are required and able to delete. Any data retained for legitimate legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons will be limited to what is necessary and retained only for the applicable period.

Contact

For account deletion questions, contact Majesty Live Support.
